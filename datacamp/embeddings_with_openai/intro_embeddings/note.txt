 Embedding models map text onto a multi-dimensional space, or vector space, and the numbers outputted by the model represent the location of the text in that space.
 More similar pieces of text or words, like teacher and student, are mapped closer together in the space and dissimilar words are mapped further away.
 This allows to capture semantic meaning i.e context and intent

Uses
 Allows for semantic search engines
 Recommendation systems
 Classification of sentiment, cluster observations
